You choose the initialization初始化由你来选

Build a small network, then decide how every single layer is initialized — a 0/1 matrix, a Gaussian matrix or a uniform matrix, each with your own mean and variance. Press Train and look at the error curves. Nothing on this page tells you which choice is right; finding that out is the experiment. 先搭一个小网络，然后逐层决定初始化方式——0/1 矩阵、高斯矩阵或均匀分布矩阵，均值与方差都自己调。按下 Train，只误差曲线。这一页不会告诉你哪种选择是对的，把它试出来就是这次实验的目的。

The only feedback is the error. We deliberately do not plot anything about the signal inside the network — at this point we have not introduced it yet. Judge an initialization by whether training and test error come down. 唯一的反馈就是误差。我们刻意不画网络内部的任何信号量——此时还没讲到它。判断一个初始化好不好，只看训练误差和测试误差有没有降下来。

1  Build the network搭建网络

A plain fully connected network for two-moon classification: no bias, no normalization, no skip connections, so that everything you see comes from the initialization. Layer 1 takes the 2-d input; every other layer takes a d-d vector. 一个用于月牙二分类的普通全连接网络：无偏置、无归一化、无跳跃连接，因此你看到的一切都只来自初始化。第 1 层接收 2 维输入，其余每层接收 d 维向量。

2  Choose the initialization of every layer逐层选择初始化

Three families are available for each layer. 0/1: every entry is 0 or 1, and you tune the fraction of ones. N(μ, σ²): i.i.d. Gaussian entries with the mean and variance you set. U(μ, σ²): i.i.d. uniform entries with the mean and variance you set. The last row is the output layer. 每一层都有三类可选。0/1：每个元素取 0 或 1，你调 1 的比例。N(μ, σ²)：独立同分布高斯，均值与方差自己设。U(μ, σ²)：独立同分布均匀分布，均值与方差自己设。最后一行是输出层。

Decision boundary决策边界

The most recent run. A well-initialized network usually shows a broad region of confident prediction, not a uniform grey. 最近一次训练的结果。初始化得当的网络通常会出现大片置信度较高的区域，而不是一片均匀的灰色。

Each run keeps its curve when you train again (up to five), so you can change one layer and compare directly. The curves show error, not loss: the fraction of misclassified points, which is what you can actually read off an axis. 每次训练都会把曲线留在图上（最多保留五条），这样可以只改一层然后直接比较。图上画的是误差而不是损失：分错点的比例，这是真正能从坐标轴上读出来的量。

Two-moons data is generated in the page from the seed; 400 training and 400 test points. Optimizer: SGD with momentum at one fixed learning rate and no gradient clipping, so runs differ only because of the initialization — a scale that is far too large is allowed to blow up, and a run whose gradients turn non-finite is marked diverged and stopped. The curves are noisy on purpose: they are what a single run actually looks like. 月牙数据由页面按种子实时生成，训练集与测试集各 400 点。优化器：带动量的 SGD，学习率固定，且不做梯度裁剪，因此各次运行的差别只来自初始化——尺度过大时就真的会炸，梯度出现非有限值的运行会被标记为 diverged 并中止。曲线有抖动是刻意的：单次训练实际就是这样。
