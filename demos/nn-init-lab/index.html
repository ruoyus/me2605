Find settings that make the network train调出一组能让网络训练起来的设置

Six fixed scenarios. In every one of them you get a network that does not train with the settings it opens with — your job is to find settings that fix it. The only feedback on this page is the training and test error, so those two curves are your whole instrument. 六个固定场景。每一个场景里的网络，用它开局的设置都训练不起来——把它调好就是你的任务。这一页只给你训练误差和测试误差，所以这两条曲线就是你全部的仪器。

An error of 50% means the network learned nothing at all — that is the chance level for this data set. A scenario is cleared once the training error reaches that scenario's target. 误差 50% 意味着网络什么都没学到——这正是在这份数据集上的瞎猜水平。当训练误差降到该场景的目标以下，这个场景就算通关。

Decision boundary决策边界

The most recent run, on the two interleaved spirals. A network that has actually learned bends the boundary around the origin. 最近一次训练的结果，数据是两条互相缠绕的螺旋。真正学到的网络，决策边界会绕着原点弯过去。

Six scenarios, and one initialization setting shared by every layer. The targets were not guessed: the initialization scale and the learning rate were swept against this exact code, and a working setting lands at a few percent while the setting the page opens with sits at the chance level (50%) on all six. 六个场景，所有层共用一处初始化设置。目标不是拍脑袋定的：初始化尺度与学习率都在这段代码上扫过一遍，能 work 的设置能到几个百分点，而页面开局那个设置在六个场景上都停在瞎猜水平（50%）。

Each run keeps its curve when you train again (up to five), so you can change one thing and compare directly. The curves show error, not loss: the fraction of misclassified points, which is what you can actually read off an axis. 900 SGD steps per run, batch 32, and no gradient clipping — a scale that is far too large is allowed to blow up, and a run whose gradients turn non-finite is marked diverged and stopped. 每次训练都会把曲线留在图上（最多保留五条），这样可以只改一处然后直接比较。图上画的是误差而不是损失：分错点的比例，这是真正能从坐标轴上读出来的量。每次运行 900 步 SGD、批大小 32、不做梯度裁剪——尺度过大时就真的会炸；梯度出现非有限值的运行会被标记为 diverged 并中止。

Training and test sets are 400 points each, generated in the page from a fixed seed. Progress is remembered in this browser only — the “reset progress” button clears it. 训练集与测试集各 400 点，由页面按固定种子生成。通关进度只记在这个浏览器里，按「清空进度」即可重置。
