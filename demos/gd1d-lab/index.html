Make gradient descent work — pick where to start, and how far to step让梯度下降跑起来——自己选起点，自己选步长

One function, one rule, and two things that are yours to choose: the starting point w₀ and the step size η. A run counts as working if the iterates reach w ≈ 1, where the function is zero. 一个函数、一条迭代规则，只有两件事由你决定：起点 w₀ 和步长 η。什么时候算成功？迭代能走到 w ≈ 1，那里函数值为 0。

The problem. Minimise F(w) = (w7 − 1)2 by gradient descent with a constant step size: w ← w − η F′(w). 问题。 用固定步长的梯度下降最小化 F(w) = (w7 − 1)2： w ← w − η F′(w)。
