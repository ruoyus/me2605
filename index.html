What is this course about?课程简介

This PhD-level course develops a principled and practical understanding of neural network training, with particular emphasis on large language models. It connects mathematical foundations, empirical analysis, and systems considerations to explain how modern models are trained, why training succeeds or fails, and how training can be made more effective and efficient. Through lectures, hands-on experiments, and an open-ended project, students learn to analyze training behavior, evaluate competing explanations, and design reproducible improvements to real training pipelines.

这门博士生课程旨在建立对神经网络训练的原则性理解与实用能力，重点放在大语言模型上。课程将数学基础、 实证分析与系统视角相结合，解释现代模型如何被训练、训练为何成功或失败，以及如何让训练更有效、更高效。 通过课堂讲授、动手实验和开放性项目，学生将学会分析训练行为、评估不同解释， 并为真实训练流程设计可复现的改进。

Prerequisites先修要求

  • Proficiency in Python and PyTorch — the coursework is implementation-heavy.
  • 熟练使用 Python 与 PyTorch —— 本课程的练习以动手实现为主。
  • College Calculus and Linear Algebra — matrix/vector notation and operations.
  • 微积分与线性代数 —— 熟悉矩阵/向量记号与运算。
  • Probability and Statistics — basics of distributions, expectation, variance.
  • 概率与统计 —— 分布、期望、方差等基础。
  • Machine Learning — basics of supervised learning and deep learning.
  • 机器学习 —— 监督学习与深度学习基础。
